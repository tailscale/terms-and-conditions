Last Updated: August 25, 2026

By using PAM, you agree to be bound to these PAM Terms. ## 1. Fees & Usage Limits

Fees for PAM, usage limits, and usage based components are described in the applicable Order Form, pricing page, rate card, or other Documentation.

## 2. PAM Data

PAM enables you to control, facilitate, monitor, and audit access by Permitted Users and End Users to servers, databases, applications, infrastructure, and other resources that you make accessible through PAM.

PAM may collect, host, store, access, or otherwise process information regarding PAM sessions and access activity, including connection metadata, audit logs, commands executed, database queries, API requests, session events, access decisions, and other activity associated with the use of protected resources. (“**PAM Activity Data**”).

If enabled by you, PAM may also collect and store user session artifacts, including screen and session recordings, transcripts, terminal output, web application activity, and related audit records (“**Session Recordings**”).  You are responsible for determining whether and how to enable monitoring and Session Recordings and for providing any notices and obtaining any consents or other authorizations required by applicable law in connection with monitoring or recording Permitted Users or End Users. 

PAM Activity Data and Session Recordings are “**PAM Data**”.

## 3. PAM Metrics

PAM collects and generates metrics and analytics regarding use and operation of PAM, including connection counts, authentication events, resource access patterns, session duration, feature utilization, performance data, and security events (“**PAM Metrics**”).

You are responsible for configuring and securing your identity providers, accounts, credentials, authentication methods, secrets managers, and resources used with PAM. You will not use PAM to provide any person with access to a resource unless you are authorized to provide that access.

## 6. AI Insights

PAM may include optional features that use artificial intelligence or machine learning to analyze your PAM Activity Data and Session Recordings and generate summaries, classifications, risk scores, alerts, recommendations, or other information regarding PAM sessions (“**AI Insights**”).  AI Insights are PAM Data.

To provide AI Insights, Tailscale may use artificial intelligence or machine learning models hosted or made available by Amazon Web Services (“AWS”) or other subprocessors identified in accordance with our DPA. By enabling or using AI Insights features, you instruct Tailscale to process PAM Activity Data and Session Recordings using such models to provide AI Insights.

AI Insights may be inaccurate, incomplete, or fail to identify relevant activity and should not be relied upon as the sole basis for security, access-control, employment, disciplinary, or other decisions that could materially affect an individual. You are responsible for evaluating AI Insights and determining whether and how to act on them.

You are solely responsible for your use of Third-Party Services with PAM.

You are responsible for exporting any PAM Data you wish to retain before expiration or termination of your access to PAM. Unless otherwise expressly agreed in writing, Tailscale has no obligation to provide access to PAM Data following expiration or termination.

Unless we expressly agree in writing, you will not configure or use PAM to submit, collect, record, store, or otherwise process data that contains: government-issued identifiers (e.g., social security numbers, driver’s license numbers, passport numbers); payment card data or bank account numbers; protected health information regulated by HIPAA; precise geolocation data; children’s personal data; or “sensitive personal data” or “special category personal data” as defined by applicable privacy laws.
